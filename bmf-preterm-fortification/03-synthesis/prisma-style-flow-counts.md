# PRISMA-style flow counts

This is a narrative (systematised) review, not a systematic review — no PROSPERO registration and no dual-screening protocol were used. These counts are reported because, per RESEARCH-BRIEF.md Section 9/11, modern editors expect flow transparency even for narrative reviews, and because an honest count is more useful than a false impression of exhaustive systematic screening.

## Identification

- **PubMed searches run:** 5 validation searches (Section 7.5 landmarks) + Domain 1 combo-2 approximation (22 hits) + approximately 25 additional targeted PubMed direct-title/author searches across Domains 2–8 (each typically 1–10 hits, used to pin down a specific named seed-list paper to a verified PMID) + several DOI-to-PMID conversion batches (not independent searches, confirmatory lookups).
- **Consensus MCP claim queries run:** 7 primary domain-framed queries (per Section 7.3 of the brief, one per domain) plus 2 targeted supplementary queries (Malcolm/AREDF, Bohnhorst/NEC-refeeding) where PubMed keyword search under-recalled a specific named author. Each Consensus query returned up to 20 ranked results.
- **Total unique records screened at title/abstract level (approximate):** ~180–220, accounting for overlap across the ~7 Consensus queries (each returning up to 20 results, with substantial cross-domain overlap — e.g. Seliga-Siwecka, Schanler and N-forte all surfaced repeatedly across Domain 1, 5 and 8 queries) and the ~30 PubMed direct searches.

## Screening and exclusion

- **Excluded as off-topic on title/abstract screen (approximate):** ~90–110 records — predominantly (a) term-infant or general-formula-modification trials with no preterm-HMF relevance (e.g., Verfürden 2021, Lucas 1998/2001, Timby 2021, Colombo 2023, Tian 2025 — encountered during the Domain 5 Consensus query and explicitly excluded as indirect evidence per Section 10's discard-and-report instruction); (b) unrelated papers by common-surname authors with no substantive topical match (e.g., several "Malcolm" and "Lamport" hits from author-name Consensus searches that were entirely unrelated cardiology/cognitive-nutrition/sports-medicine papers); (c) duplicate/overlapping trial reports already captured via a more specific search.
- **Excluded after closer read as indirect/wrong-intervention (retained for discussion, not extraction):** the Cochrane "nutrient-enriched formula following hospital discharge" line (Young/Walsh 2016, Henderson 2016) — a different intervention (formula, not human-milk fortification) explicitly discussed in Domain 7 to prevent the conflation the brief warns against, but not extracted as fortification-of-human-milk evidence in the main decision table.

## Full-text assessed

- **Full-text or structured-abstract review performed specifically to check for a stated fortifier-specific recommendation (Domain 3):** 2 documents (ERNICA 2024 guideline, PMID 39925108; Italian SIN/SICP/SINUPE 2026 position paper, PMID 41519795) — both confirmed, on full-text-level review of their recommendation domains, to contain no fortifier-specific timing/dosing statement distinct from general enteral-feeding advice.
- **Abstract-only extraction (marked `[abstract only]` in extraction.csv where applicable):** Tucker 2025 (PMID 40714049) is marked abstract-only in the extraction table; full-text access was not independently verified for several other entries where PubMed's abstract-level metadata was judged sufficient to extract the numbers required (all such numbers are transcribed as reported in the abstract, per the brief's non-negotiable rule, and not re-derived).

## Included in extraction table

- **Total records included in `02-extraction/extraction.csv`:** 49, spanning all 8 domains (Domain 1: 7; Domain 2: 5; Domain 3: 4; Domain 4: 3; Domain 5: 6; Domain 6: 5; Domain 7: 5; Domain 8: 14). Domain totals sum to 49; several individual studies are cross-referenced across two domains in the synthesis narrative (e.g., Klamer 2022 appears in both Domain 5 and Domain 7 discussion; Rochow 2025's target-fortification ND trial appears in both Domain 5 and Domain 8) but are extracted only once in the CSV under their primary domain to avoid double-counting the same trial as independent evidence.
