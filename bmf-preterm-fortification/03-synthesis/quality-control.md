1. **Combo 12 (design-filtered mechanical re-runs of combos 2–10)** was not executed as a separate, literal re-run for every domain given the scale of the brief; study-design screening was instead applied at the point of extraction (RCTs/Cochrane reviews/meta-analyses prioritised, observational evidence retained only where RCT evidence was absent). 2. **Combination 11** (I AND G2, no population filter, SR/RCT-restricted) was not run as a standalone mechanical PubMed string; its intended yield was instead captured incidentally through the Domain 5 Consensus query, which surfaced 7 term/mixed-population papers, all explicitly excluded as indirect. 3. **English-language filtering** was not explicitly applied as a separate PubMed filter parameter; all retrieved records were in English by inspection, but this was not independently confirmed as an active search constraint — the brief's instruction to treat this as a stated limitation rather than a neutral default is honoured here.
4. **Full-text retrieval** was performed selectively (Domain 3's two guideline/position-paper documents, specifically to check for a fortifier-specific statement) rather than systematically across all 49 included records; most extraction relies on PubMed abstract-level metadata, consistent with the brief's `[abstract only]` marking convention, which has been applied where relevant.
