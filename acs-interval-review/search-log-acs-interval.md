## Subagent search logs (Q2–Q6)

All five subagents completed and reported on 2026-08-27. Each ran PubMed `search_articles`/`get_article_metadata`/`get_full_text_article`/`find_related_articles`, 1-2 Consensus-style `search` calls, and WebSearch/WebFetch for guideline text. Aggregate: 203 tool calls, ~860,000 subagent tokens. Every PMID/DOI reported was returned directly from a PubMed metadata lookup; each agent's own "could not verify" list is consolidated into the master synthesis document's final section.

- **Q2 (pharmacokinetics)**: retrieved and verified Tsuei 1980 (PMID 7389259), Jobe 2019 (PMID 31808984), Petersen 1983 (PMID 6662178), Samtani 2005 I/II (PMID 16371449/16371448), Blanford 1977 (PMID 835623), van der Heijden 2024 (PMID 38763343), Ballard 1996 (PMID 8652188), Venkatesh 1993 (PMID 8427712), plus Consensus-sourced Krzyzanski 2023, Morsi 2018, and GR-transcription mechanistic papers. Key correction identified: UK betamethasone is the plain phosphate ester, not the US acetate-depot mixture the brief assumed.
- **Q3 (clinical interval evidence)**: six independent PubMed search strategies confirmed no dexamethasone-interval RCT exists; retrieved Khandelwal 2012 (PMID 22381601, the only betamethasone-interval RCT), Brownfoot 2013 Cochrane (PMID 23990333), Daskalakis 2023 European guideline (PMID 36689999), WHO ACTION-I protocol (PMID 31420064), Rasool 2017 pilot (PMID 29076668), and Chawanpaiboon 2023 dose-comparison RCT (PMID 37442247, found independently by lead session).
- **Q4 (imminent delivery)**: retrieved Karmoker 2020 (PMID 31915337), McDougall 2023 WHO systematic review (PMID 37007738), Kraft 2025 (PMID 41014996), Gulersen 2021 (PMID 34153514), Edut 2026 (PMID 41753292), Li 2026 (PMID 41872805), Wu 2026 (PMID 41495663), Mwita 2023 (PMID 37991049), Saldaña-García 2021/2022 (PMID 35011761/35268318), Ng 2002 (PMID 12364445), Schäffer 2009 (PMID 19384124), Zigron 2021 (PMID 34625970), plus Consensus-sourced mechanistic papers (Gross 1983, Liley 1988, Ballard 1972/1995/1997).
- **Q5 (maternal hyperglycaemia/adrenal)**: retrieved Jones 2025 scoping review (PMID 40935291), Shareef 2025 (PMID 40473019), Hong/PROMAC 2021 (PMID 33588801), Kaushal 2003 (PMID 12519324), Tuohy 2021 (PMID 33600450), Roberts 2017/McGoldrick 2020 Cochrane (PMID 28321847/33368142), Vermillion 1999 (PMID 10454676), McKenna 2004/2000 (PMID 15370080/10992191), Koenen 2005 (PMID 15774821).
- **Q6 (long-term neurodevelopment)**: verified ASTEROID (PMID 31523039) and Cochrane-extracted breakdown (PMID 35943347) exact figures matching the brief's cerebral palsy CI precisely; retrieved Carter 2024 sheep study (PMID 39183288, confirming PMID left unresolved by the Q3 agent), MACS/MACS-5 (PMID 19101390/20956409/24126948), ACTORDS (PMID 16765760/17881750/27650051), Wapner NICHD (PMID 17881751), DART (PMID 16396863/17403842), Yeh 2004 (PMID 15044641), Wilson-Costello 2009 (PMID 19204058).

## Phase 7 — verification of user-supplied reference material (2026-08-27)

Per the user's explicit instruction ("if can not find some thing do not state in document"), every claim from the HTML infographic not already in this project's verified evidence base was checked against PubMed before use.
