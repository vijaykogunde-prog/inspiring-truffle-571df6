# References

Every entry below carries a PMID and/or DOI retrieved and verified against PubMed this session (20 Sep 2026). Per CLAUDE.md rule 1, nothing appears here that was not independently verified; items that could not be verified are excluded and logged in `could-not-verify.md` instead. Full bibliographic detail (complete author lists, volume/page numbers) is given where it was captured during extraction; where a domain synthesis file recorded only the short author/year form with a verified PMID/DOI, that is what appears below rather than an invented full citation — see the limitation noted in `../QC-report.md`. Numbering is by domain/study id as used throughout `../02-extraction/extraction.csv` and `../03-synthesis/`, not final journal-submission Vancouver numbering (that renumbering, if needed for the *Infant* manuscript, is a `reference-builder`-skill task, not done here).

Duplicate studies extracted under more than one domain (e.g. Nasseri 2006 in both Domain A and B; Okulu 2022, Al-Lawama 2019, Miqdad 2004, and Gutiérrez-Vélez 2022 each appearing in two domains' extraction rows) are listed once here, with all their extraction-table ids noted.
