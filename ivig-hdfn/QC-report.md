## Additional limitations disclosed (not on the original checklist, but material)

1. **Network egress was blocked for essentially every primary grey-literature domain** attempted by the Domain E and F researchers (nice.org.uk, wiley, medicines.org.uk, sciencedirect.com, b-s-h.org.uk, cps.ca, pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, england.nhs.uk, nhs.scot, ema.europa.eu). This is the single biggest constraint on this review's grey-literature confidence and is flagged at the top of `domain-F.md`, in `guidance-table.md`'s header note, and in the unit guideline draft's status box. **The guideline group should not ratify the draft guideline without directly obtaining and reading the NICE CG98 full evidence review and the current UK IVIG product SPC(s).**
2. **Bibliographic completeness in `references.md` is uneven.** Where a domain synthesis file recorded only an author/year/PMID/DOI (rather than a full journal/volume/page citation), that is what appears in the reference list — no missing bibliographic detail was invented to make entries look more complete than what was actually retrieved and recorded this session.
3. **Full-text retrieval was the exception, not the rule.** The great majority of the 56 extraction rows are marked `abstract_only = Y`, meaning most numeric detail (especially confidence intervals) comes from what PubMed abstracts report, which frequently omit CIs even when p-values or point estimates are given. This is marked field-by-field in `extraction.csv` (many `ci_lower`/`ci_upper` cells are empty rather than estimated) rather than backfilled with derived numbers, per rule 2.
4. **Seed literature (§10) verification outcome:** all leads chased were confirmed to exist and to say approximately what the brief described; §10 was a well-informed scoping list, not a source of any false leads that needed discarding. The 10 items that were discarded during the full search (see `could-not-verify.md`) were all leads surfaced independently during the Domains E/F searches themselves (mostly via Consensus MCP), not from the brief's own §10 list.
