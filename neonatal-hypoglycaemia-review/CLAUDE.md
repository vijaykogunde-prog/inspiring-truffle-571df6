# Project standing rules

Read this at the start of every session.

## Project
- Question: How should hypoglycaemia be defined, detected, and managed in term and preterm infants in the first days of life and beyond, and what evidence underpins current UK (BAPM) practice? (see RESEARCH-BRIEF.md for full scope, secondary questions A–G, and running instructions)
- Track: narrative evidence synthesis — governance/teaching document for internal unit practice, NOT a manuscript by default
- Target journal: none (internal BAPM/NICE-framed governance synthesis, Imperial College Healthcare NHS Trust Level 3 NICU). If a manuscript is later wanted, hand to medical-academic-researcher or this pipeline's phase 5 with a named journal.
- PROSPERO ID (if applicable): n/a — narrative synthesis, not registered

## Clinical framing defaults
- UK primary: BAPM, NICE, RCPCH.
- Dominant RCT evidence base is NZ (CHYLD, Sugar Babies, hPOD/pre-hPOD) in late-preterm/term infants — flag every extrapolation to preterm/very preterm populations explicitly.
- Author/reader: consultant neonatologist, UK Level 3 NICU.
- Units: report mmol/L and mg/dL together always (1 mmol/L = 18.0 mg/dL; 2.6 mmol/L = 47 mg/dL; 2.0 mmol/L = 36 mg/dL).

## Non-negotiables
- Nothing enters any file without a verified PMID or DOI retrieved this session.
- Unverifiable references are removed and listed, never hedged into the text.
- Numbers are transcribed as reported, never re-derived or rounded. Mark `[abstract only]` where full text was unavailable.
- Every interventional claim carries a GRADE label with a one-line reason naming the downgrade domain.
- Every claim flags whether evidence is direct or extrapolated.
- Null and non-replication findings get equal prominence to positive ones.
- Write findings to disk incrementally. Never hold a phase in context alone.
- Append every search string, source, date and hit count to 01-search-log/searches.md as it runs.
- Say plainly when the evidence is weak. Do not hedge into vagueness.
