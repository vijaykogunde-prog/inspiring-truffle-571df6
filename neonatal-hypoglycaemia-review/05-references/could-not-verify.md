## Access limitation, not a citation failure — flagged for transparency

**bapm.org itself could not be directly retrieved by any domain this session.** Every `WebFetch` attempt to `www.bapm.org` (and, for Domain A specifically, also `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` and `www.mdpi.com` when accessed by direct URL rather than via the PubMed MCP connector) returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`. This did not cause any peer-reviewed reference to fail verification, but it means every BAPM-specific numeric threshold reported anywhere in this synthesis is transcribed from peer-reviewed secondary characterisations (principally Giouleka et al. 2023, PMID 37508719, and Rusu et al. 2026, PMID 42194883) or from web-search-indexed page titles/snippets and NHS trust guidelines citing BAPM verbatim (Domains B, C, G), not from bapm.org's primary text retrieved directly this session. The secondary sources are mutually consistent where they overlap (48h persistent-hypoglycaemia boundary; general pathway structure), which supports transcription accuracy but does not substitute for primary verification. **This is the single most important limitation of the entire synthesis and should be the first thing re-checked if bapm.org access is available from a different network environment** — see the cross-cutting synthesis and Domain A/B/C files for detail.

## Non-existent trial name, corrected rather than a failed citation

The research brief named a "COIN trial" for preterm insulin therapy (Domain G scope). No trial by that name exists in the peer-reviewed literature — Domain G ran a dedicated negative search confirming this. The trial the brief was almost certainly describing is the correctly-named **NIRTURE trial** (Beardsall et al. 2008, *NEJM*, PMID 18971490, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa0803725), which is fully verified and used throughout Domain G's synthesis under its correct name. This is recorded here as a transparent correction to the brief itself, not as an unverifiable citation quietly dropped.
